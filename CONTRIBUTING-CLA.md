
Corporate contributors should contact the maintainer to sign a
Corporate CLA instead.

## 7. Why a CLA on top of AGPL-3.0

The CLA grants explicit rights that allow the project to:

1. **Re-license** to compatible future copyleft licenses
   (e.g., AGPL-4.0 once published) without chasing down every
   historical contributor.
2. **Dual-license** if the project ever needs to offer a commercial
   exception for downstream proprietary integrations (subject to
   community consultation — there is no plan to do so today).

The CLA does NOT grant the project the right to release Your
Contribution under a non-copyleft license without your separate
written consent.
